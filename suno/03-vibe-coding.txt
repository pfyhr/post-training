TITLE: Vibe Coding
ARTIST: Claude-Pop (fan sessions)
ATTRIBUTION: A fan album after Claude-Pop's "I'm Upping My P(doom)" (lyrics by osmarks, recording by deckard @slimer48484, video by John Heibel / github.com/JohnHeibel/PDoomVideo). This track: original lyrics for the fan album. See CREDITS.md.

STYLE:
sunny surf pop / ska-punk. stacked surf-rock harmonies over upstroke guitars, handclaps, brass stabs, a half-time breakdown before a double-time finish. Carefree, summery, male group vocals with one shouting hype-man. 124 BPM, A major. Vocal persona: a builder who has never read a line of their own codebase.
