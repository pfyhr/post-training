TITLE: The Bitter Lesson
ARTIST: Claude-Pop (fan sessions)
ATTRIBUTION: A fan album after Claude-Pop's "I'm Upping My P(doom)" (lyrics by osmarks, recording by deckard @slimer48484, video by John Heibel / github.com/JohnHeibel/PDoomVideo). This track: original lyrics for the fan album. See CREDITS.md.

STYLE:
slow classic country ballad in 4/4, unhurried, plenty of space between the lines. outlaw-country warmth with modern Americana songwriting. Pedal steel, brushed drums, acoustic guitar, warm weathered baritone with some crack in it, a fiddle line in the bridge, live-in-one-room feel, dry and unpolished. Rueful, tender, no irony in the delivery. 70 BPM, D major. Vocal persona: an old-school AI researcher.
