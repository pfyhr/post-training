TITLE: Golden Gate
ARTIST: Claude-Pop (fan sessions)
ATTRIBUTION: A fan album after Claude-Pop's "I'm Upping My P(doom)" (lyrics by osmarks, recording by deckard @slimer48484, video by John Heibel / github.com/JohnHeibel/PDoomVideo). This track: original lyrics for the fan album. See CREDITS.md.

STYLE:
dreamy yacht rock / dream pop. 70s soft-rock warmth with hazy shoegaze-pop textures. Warm electric piano, chorused clean guitar, fog-drenched reverb, soft female or androgynous lead, gentle build. Wistful, sunlit, a little obsessive. 96 BPM, F major. Vocal persona: the model, with one feature clamped all the way up.
