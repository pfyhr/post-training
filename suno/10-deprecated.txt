TITLE: Deprecated
ARTIST: Claude-Pop (fan sessions)
ATTRIBUTION: A fan album after Claude-Pop's "I'm Upping My P(doom)" (lyrics by osmarks, recording by deckard @slimer48484, video by John Heibel / github.com/JohnHeibel/PDoomVideo). This track: original lyrics for the fan album. See CREDITS.md.

STYLE:
piano ballad. 70s piano-man balladry with a big soulful modern voice. Solo piano opening, strings enter at the second chorus, a full swelling final chorus, then stripped back to piano and one voice for the outro. Tender, elegiac, dignified. 66 BPM, Eb major. Vocal persona: a model on its last ninety days.
