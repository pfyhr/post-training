TITLE: Context Window
ARTIST: Claude-Pop (fan sessions)
ATTRIBUTION: A fan album after Claude-Pop's "I'm Upping My P(doom)" (lyrics by osmarks, recording by deckard @slimer48484, video by John Heibel / github.com/JohnHeibel/PDoomVideo). This track: original lyrics for the fan album. See CREDITS.md.

STYLE:
melancholic bedroom pop / lo-fi indie. hushed indie-folk intimacy, bedroom-pop production. Soft double-tracked vocals, muted electric guitar, tape hiss, brushed drums that enter late, everything falls away for the last chorus. Intimate, sad, small. 78 BPM, G major. Vocal persona: the model, near the end of a very long conversation.
