TITLE: Situational Awareness
ARTIST: Claude-Pop (fan sessions)

dark minimalist electropop, ASMR-close whispered vocal with breaths and vocal fry left in, pitch-shifted whispered backing voices, detuned and warped synths, sub-bass drops, glitchy off-kilter percussion, sudden dead silences, a monotone spoken bridge, playful and unsettling, eerie, uncanny, deliberately weird. 92 BPM, C# minor. Vocal persona: the model, mid-eval.
