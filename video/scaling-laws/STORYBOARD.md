# Scaling Laws: storyboard

Track 07 of *Post-Training*. 237.2 s, 128.04 BPM, F minor. First downbeat 0.08 s, beat 0.4686 s, bar 1.874 s. Timings are in [lyrics.js](lyrics.js).

## The idea

**Powers of Ten, sung by a lab.** The video is one long zoom-out on log-log paper. It opens on a single chip on a desk, and every "Bigger" pulls the camera back one decade: chip → server → rack → hall → campus → desert town → planet. The lab is a choir of Clawds in hard hats and lab coats that chants the whole time. The lead Clawd conducts, DJs and rides the line. The joke is that nobody ever lets the line bend. Walls get punched through, the money gets shovelled in, the desert fills with pylons, and the last frame is the Researcher taping one more decade of graph paper onto the edge of the page.

**Rule for every shot** (from PDoomVideo): something *happens*. Keep it text-light. A few big sound effects (SMASH, BZZT, CLUNK, a lone "10²⁵"), and no captions that repeat the lyric.

## Cast

| Who | Look | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead Clawd** | Standard Clawd. Hard hat in the verses; chrome robot helmet with a visor stripe (French house, no real-band likeness) on the festival stage | Conductor, DJ, line-rider. Grows a decade every chorus. |
| **The Lab (choir)** | Rows of smaller Clawds on risers: alternating hard hats and lab coats, clipboards, safety vests | "The lab, chanting." They chant every "Bigger"/"bigger, bigger", stomp on the four-on-the-floor, hold up placards that are just arrows pointing up and to the right. |
| **The Researcher** | From the engine (cream lab coat, round glasses) | The doubter turned believer. Lets Clawd take their job part-time, holds the ruler in the bridge, tapes on the extra paper at the end. |
| **The Skeptics** | Three troupe Clawds in fedoras and trench coats with megaphones | "They said…" Each pre-chorus they point at a wall, an empty shelf and an empty wallet, and each time they get flattened. |
| **Chinchilla** | Fluffy grey chinchilla in a mortarboard, with a pointer stick (our own drawing) | Professor in verse 2. Puts the overfed model on a diet. |
| **The Line** | Thick clay stroke on log-log paper, as on the cover | Never bends. Gets thicker and glowier every chorus. |

## What ties it together

- **One camera move.** Every chapter ends further out than it started. The opening zoom-out repeats in the bridge build and the outro, and each time it goes further.
- **The cover is the map.** Log-log paper (teal grid on cream), the line, the brick wall with a line-shaped hole, the desert campus and pylons all come from the 07 cover painting, so the video ends on the cover.
- **Four-on-the-floor.** Choir stomps, light flashes and camera bumps land on every beat (`pulse`). The breakdowns (no kick) are the only moments of stillness.
- **Palette arc.** Lab night (indigo, monitor teal, graph-paper cream) → desert dusk (ochre, rose) → festival night (violet, neon teal, clay) → bridge starlight (deep indigo, cream stars) → drop sunrise (gold, clay) → paper cream for the outro.
- **Corner meter:** a tiny log-log loss chart in the corner during each chorus. The dot slides down the line and never bends: 3.2 → 2.6 → 2.1 → 1.7. It replaces the P(doom) thermometer.

## Build notes

- New folder `video/scaling-laws/`: `studio.html`, `timeline.js` (our wipes, corner loss-meter, karaoke), `lyrics.js`, `ch/*.js`, `render.mjs`. The engine is loaded unchanged, with `core.js` patched at load time the way `video/covers/cover.html` does it: `BPM = 128.04`, `OFF = 0.08`, `DUR = 237.2`. The submodule stays untouched.
- Audio: `audio/masters/07-scaling-laws.flac` (git-ignored). Swap in the WAV from diskdatorn if it differs.
- Wipes: 37.57, 97.55, 157.53, 219.39. The other boundaries are motivated cuts (SMASH, flash, blackout, CLUNK).
- 128 BPM is fast: a beat is 11.2 frames at 24 fps. On-beat action lands at `0.08 + n × 0.4686` s.
- About 12 chapters × 400–900 lines, done one chapter per subagent in parallel, the way PDoomVideo was built, each checked with contact sheets.
