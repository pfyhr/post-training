# 04 · Golden Gate

**Style:** dreamy yacht rock / dream pop. 70s soft-rock warmth with hazy shoegaze-pop textures. Warm electric piano, chorused clean guitar, fog-drenched reverb, soft female or androgynous lead, gentle build. Wistful, sunlit, a little obsessive.
**Tempo:** 96 BPM · **Key:** F major · **Length:** ~3:45
**Voice:** the model, with one feature clamped all the way up.
