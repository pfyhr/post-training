# 09 · The Bitter Lesson

**Style:** slow classic country ballad in 4/4, unhurried, plenty of space between the lines. outlaw-country warmth with modern Americana songwriting. Pedal steel, brushed drums, acoustic guitar, warm weathered baritone with some crack in it, a fiddle line in the bridge, live-in-one-room feel, dry and unpolished. Rueful, tender, no irony in the delivery.
**Tempo:** 70 BPM · **Key:** D major · **Length:** ~4:00
**Voice:** an old-school AI researcher.

---

[Verse 1]
I taught it every opening, the Sicilian and the French
Hand-tuned every table, hunched over on that bench
Then a box outsearched Kasparov, I said "that's not chess"
Then one learned it in an afternoon, and beat the box, no less

[Chorus]
It's the bitter lesson, darlin', and it tastes just like it sounds
Everything I hand-carved got run into the ground
Search and learning, learning and search
My life's work is a footnote, filed under "prior research"
It's the bitter lesson, and I keep forgetting that I learned

[Verse 2]
I wrote a rule for every phoneme, every vowel we've got
Then they fired all the linguists, and the error rate just dropped

Then two gamer cards saw a million cats, and that was that

Moore's law is a river, and I built my house on the bank
Every spring the water's higher, and I've got compute to thank
Rich put it in a blog post, I stapled it to my wall

[Verse 3 – quieter]

They just add another zero and they watch the losses go
And I'm proud of them, I swear it, but some nights I still pretend

Every time I fired a linguist, the thing got better.
Every time.

**References:** Rich Sutton's 2019 essay "The Bitter Lesson", posted on his blog: general methods that scale with compute (search and learning) beat human-knowledge engineering, in chess, Go, speech and vision · Deep Blue out-searching Kasparov in 1997 and the "that's not real chess" reaction · AlphaZero teaching itself chess by self-play in hours and beating the hand-tuned engines · Fred Jelinek's line "every time I fire a linguist, the performance of the speech recognizer goes up" · hand-crafted vision features (edges, corners, SIFT) giving way to AlexNet in 2012, trained on two gaming GPUs in Toronto · AlphaGo's move 37 · "add another zero", a nod to track 07 · Moore's law as the tide.
