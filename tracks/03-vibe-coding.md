**Style:** sunny surf pop / ska-punk. stacked surf-rock harmonies over upstroke guitars, handclaps, brass stabs, a half-time breakdown before a double-time finish. Carefree, summery, male group vocals with one shouting hype-man.
**Tempo:** 124 BPM · **Key:** A major · **Length:** ~3:00
**Voice:** a builder who has never read a line of their own codebase.
