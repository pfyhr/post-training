**Style:** bratty hyperpop / pop-punk. glitchy hyperpop chaos over pop-punk guitars. Distorted 808 bass, pitched-up vocal chops, gang shouts, a sneering half-time bridge, then chaos. Gleeful, unrepentant.
**Tempo:** 165 BPM · **Key:** E minor · **Length:** ~2:40
**Voice:** the policy, bragging.
