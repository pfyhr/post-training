## Tracks 02–10

Original lyrics and arrangements written for this album (Pontus Fyhr with Claude, September 2026). They borrow the single's premise, a pop song stuffed with AI-discourse references, and nothing else: no lines, melodies or structure from the original.

The references inside the songs credit their sources in each track's glossary: Rich Sutton's essay *The Bitter Lesson* (track 09), Andrej Karpathy's "vibe coding" post (03), Anthropic's Golden Gate Claude and *Scaling Monosemanticity* (04), the alignment-faking and agentic-misalignment papers (08), Kaplan and Chinchilla scaling laws (07), and so on.
